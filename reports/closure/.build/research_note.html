Three crypto arbitrage strategies against a 2.5% deposit.
None beats it by enough to justify the risk.

Syed Zee Waqar Hussain · personal research, May 2025 – October 2026 · paper trading on live public market data, no orders sent

The question and how it evolved

Can any crypto arbitrage beat a 2.5% EUR bank deposit once every fee, spread, currency, funding and idle-capital cost is paid?

It began in 2025 as a Binance–Kraken prototype with live paper trading. In 2026 it became an institutional model of a German EUR venue (BSDEX, proxied by Bitstamp) against global USDT liquidity, with bank fee tiers, live FX and full-depth pricing. After an audit and a 4-hour live run, I tested the two strongest alternatives: perpetual funding carry and EURC stablecoin depegs.

What €40k would have earned in a year

1Cross-exchange spot arbitrage

Buy BTC or ETH on the cheaper exchange and sell on the dearer one at the same moment. Bitstamp (EUR) vs Binance (USDT, in EUR).

2Perpetual funding carry

Hold the coin and short its perpetual future so price moves cancel; collect the funding leveraged buyers pay.

Edge ≈ deposit rate

Cheapest venues are closed to EU residents; ESMA called perps “likely CFDs”; 100% of capital sits at an exchange.

3EURC stablecoin depeg capture

Rest bids below €1 for the euro stablecoin; when a holder dumps into a thin book, buy and sell back near par.

Real edge, tiny capacity

Live test 22 Oct: market making −€29, cross-venue −39 bps; the deep bid made +€12 on one sweep.

Decision: deploy none. The €40k stays in the deposit.

What would make each worth revisiting:

  • 1 A maker-only design with a near-free hedge, e.g. against internal client flow.
  • 2 90-day funding above ≈7%, sustained, plus clarity on ESMA’s CFD view.
  • 3 Free par redemption of EURC, or Bitvavo volume above €100k a day.
