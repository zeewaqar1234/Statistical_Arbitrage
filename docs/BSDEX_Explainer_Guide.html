Cross-venue crypto stat-arb: what it does and why it doesn't pay

The tool watches the same coin (BTC, ETH) on a EUR exchange and a USDT exchange. When the gap between them is unusually wide and wider than all costs, it buys the cheap side and sells the rich side at the same time.

03The quantitative framework

1 · Spread

s = VWAPsell − VWAPbuy

The tradeable gap for the full size. Binance prices become EUR as bid ÷ EURUSDT ask and ask ÷ EURUSDT bid, the worse side each time.

2 · Z-score

z = (s − μ) / σ

How unusual the gap is. μ = average gap, σ = its typical swing, over the window. z = 2 means "two typical swings away from normal".

3 · Cost gate

Psell(1−fs) > Pbuy(1+fb) + FX

After both taker fees and the 1 bp FX cost, the sale must still beat the purchase. This is the gate that decides.

4 · P&L per trade

PnL = q(Psell − Pbuy) − fees − FX

q = capital ÷ price. Prices are the fill prices 300 ms (live) or one bar (backtest) after the signal.

5 · Carry (funding cost)

carry = capital × 4 × 2.50% × hours / 8760

Four pre-funded pools (EUR and coin on each venue) earn nothing while waiting. 2.50% = ECB deposit rate assumption. €191.78 per week at €100k.

6 · Sharpe

mean(daily r) / sd(daily r) × √365

Shown only with ≥ 30 trades and ≥ 20 days, idle days included, so a short lucky run can't print a big number.

04Why a self-adjusting threshold, and what it did

The idea

A fixed bar fires too often when the gap is trending (it keeps looking "unusual" while it walks away) and too rarely in calm, mean-reverting markets. The live bar rises when the spread is drifting and relaxes when it is calm.

What the code does

T = clip(0.4 × 0.59 × (1+0.05·vol) × (1+0.1·|mom|), 0.3, 2.0)

0.4 × 0.59 = 0.24, below the 0.3 floor, so in calm markets T sits at 0.3σ: a low bar.

What this shows

On ETH candles the gap partly survives one minute (0.22% at signal, 0.12% at fill, >$20M tier), but fees of €170 per trade exceed the €124 average gross. The few mid-price winners need the top fee tiers and zero crossing cost. The tradeable order books never offered such a gap in 4 hours.

Data quality: 99.99% valid snapshots; one ~15-minute interruption on the test machine. Binance's 20-level feed ran out of depth for ETH at €100k in 10% of snapshots and at €250k in 63%.

09Backtest history: before and after the integrity fixes

Bug 1 · look-ahead

The gate read the fill bar, so it only took trades it already knew would win: 100% wins, zero drawdown.

Bug 2 · wrong FX

Live used the once-a-day ECB rate. Measured 14 Oct: EUR/USDT and ECB EUR/USD were 44 bps apart.

Bug 3 · instant fills

Paper fills had zero delay, so they could never lose. Now 300 ms and full-depth VWAP.

The one-line answer

The market is already arbitraged to within a few basis points. A strategy that pays the spread and fees can't win; one that earns them might.
